How to buy $TSR

Four steps. The fourth one is the one people skip and regret.

  1. Get a wallet

    Any Base-compatible wallet works: Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, MetaMask. Write the seed phrase down, not in a screenshot.

  2. Put ETH on Base

    Bridge from Ethereum or withdraw straight to Base from an exchange that supports it. You need a little extra for gas.

  3. Open the basestonk page

FAQ

Questions, answered dryly

What is TSR?
Tibbir Reserve is a token on Base. Hold it and you accumulate $TIBBIR from the fees on TSR trades. Part of the same fee burns TSR, so the supply only goes one way.
How do I receive TIBBIR?
Reflections come from the fee routing in the contract. Whether they land in your wallet automatically or need a claim is stated on the basestonk page. Check there before you assume.
Why burn?
Burned TSR goes to the dead address and never comes back. Fewer TSR in existence means each remaining TSR is a larger slice of future reflections. Simple, boring, permanent.
Is the dev supply locked?
Yes. The dev buy of 47.71M TSR (4.8% of supply) is locked from 6 Sept 2026 to 6 Sept 2027, with 0% released as of 29 Sept 2026. The countdown at the top runs to the unlock date.
What are the fees?
3.3% on buys and 3.3% on sells. The fee is what funds the TIBBIR reflections and the TSR burn. No fee, no reserve.
Where can I trade?
On the basestonk page, or through any Base DEX aggregator that routes to the pool. Chart on DexScreener. Verify the CA either way.
